Spoils of the arena
Each character carries one piece of equipment. Gear can change the blow they land, the blow they suffer, or both. On a killing strike, the attacker takes the victim’s gear and leaves their own in the sand. The brawl still ends when one character is alive, and the program prints that name.

Item	Effect
ShortSword  Outgoing attack + 10.
Greataxe  Outgoing attack × 2.
TowerShield  Incoming amount − 10, and never below 0.
